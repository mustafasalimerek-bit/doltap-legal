PrivacyTerms & Chat+Community rules
These rules apply to the player chat in every Erek Studio game — in Doltap, the chat on iPhone from version 1.4.0. The chat is a place to talk with other players. Be someone others are glad to talk to.

Zero tolerance

There is zero tolerance for the following, and no warning is owed before we act on it:

  • Harassment, bullying, threats, or encouraging anyone to hurt themselves.
  • Hate or slurs against anyone for who they are.
  • Sexual content, or anything sexual involving minors in any form.
  • Scams, spam, advertising, or selling.
  • Asking anyone for money, gift cards, codes or investments.
  • Sharing phone numbers, email addresses, links, or accounts on other apps in rooms or profiles, or anyone else's details anywhere. In an accepted one-to-one chat, Chat+ members may share their own once the other person has written at least three messages, unless that person has turned it off.
  • Pretending to be someone else.
  • Using the chat if you are under 18 (under 19 in Korea).

What happens when a rule is broken

Messages are checked automatically when they are sent and again by an AI model from Anthropic. A message that breaks a rule is not delivered or is withdrawn. Repeated breaks mute the account for 24 hours, and serious or repeated breaks end in a permanent ban. A message that three different people report is hidden automatically. Every report is acted on within 24 hours.

Player rooms

Rooms that players create follow every rule above, and so do their names and descriptions. A room's owner may remove anyone from their room; a removed person cannot rejoin it. Anyone can report a room with the flag in its menu; a room that three different people report is hidden, and rooms used to break the rules are closed and their owners' accounts handled as above.

Report and block

Tap the flag on any message or profile to report it; it disappears for you at once. Blocking someone hides you from each other and closes your conversation. Both are always free of charge and work immediately. A first message from someone new arrives as a request, and they cannot write again until you reply.

Your account

You can delete your chat account at any time in the chat: Settings → Delete chat account. What we keep and for how long is in the privacy policy; the chat's terms and Chat+ are in the terms.
