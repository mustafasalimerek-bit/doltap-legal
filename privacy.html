Short version: Doltap has no ads, analytics, or tracking. Your wish and journal stay on your device. Doltap has two optional online features: one shared weekly board (from version 1.3.1), and a player chat shared with other Erek Studio games such as Orbion (on iPhone from version 1.4.0, on Android from version 1.4.1). Nothing is sent to either until you choose to join. On the board your runs appear under a five-digit stone number, not a name; in the chat you sign in with Apple (iPhone) or Google (Android) and choose a nickname. You can leave the board and delete your board data from Settings, and delete your chat account inside the chat or as described on the account deletion page.

What we collect

The shared board. If you choose to join it, Erek Studio processes a server-issued install ID, a five-digit stone number, and your verified best tower heights. A finished run is sent as its stone order and tap timing so our server can replay it. The replay input is processed in memory and is not stored. The board does not receive your name, email, wish, journal, store account, advertising identifier, or payment information.

The player chat (iPhone 1.4.0 and later, Android 1.4.1 and later). If you choose to use it, we process your chat account, profile, messages and the safety data described in Player chat below.

Chat+. When you buy or restore the Chat+ subscription, our server confirms the purchase with Apple or Google Play; see Chat+ purchases.

Doltap contains no advertising, analytics, attribution, or crash-reporting SDK. It does not track you, and it does not use board or chat data for advertising or analytics.

Data stored on your device

Doltap saves a few things locally so the app remembers your play:

  • Your one-word wish, wish-cairn count, and best tower height.
  • Your wish journal: the date, height, wish, and stone-order number of recent runs.
  • Your season, sound, haptics, board choice, and rating-prompt timing.
  • Whether you have purchased the one-time unlock.
  • How many of your 20 free runs you have used (from version 1.3.1), and whether you have seen the one-time notice that version 1.4.0 shows to players who stacked before 1.3.1. This count is never uploaded.
  • If you join the board, its install credential, stone number, confirmed bests, and any run or deletion waiting for a connection.
  • On iPhone, if you use the chat or buy Chat+ (1.4.0 and later): your chat sign-in tokens, kept in the iOS Keychain; and the confirmed Chat+ status with its expiry date — for a purchase made without a chat account, also the App Store's signed record of the purchase — so the perks keep working offline until that date and no longer.
  • On Android, if you use the chat or buy Chat+ (1.4.1 and later): your chat sign-in tokens, kept in Android's encrypted storage and left out of device backups; and the confirmed Chat+ status with its expiry date, so the perks keep working offline until that date and no longer.

This local information is removed if you delete the app (the iOS Keychain may keep the chat sign-in tokens until you sign out or delete your chat account; on Android they go with the app). Your wish and journal are never uploaded to the shared board or the chat.

Shared board (version 1.3.1 and later)

The board combines participating players on one weekly list. Joining is optional. The first time you join, the server issues a random install ID, secret, and five-digit stone number. The secret stays on your device; the server stores only its SHA-256 hash. The ID links your weekly best and your best height since the shared board began.

For each eligible finished run, Doltap sends the week's stone-order seed, tap ticks, and claimed height. The server replays the run and ranks only the height its own replay reaches. The seed and tap ticks are not stored on the server. The daily sweep at 04:00 UTC keeps the current week and the previous 11 weeks (12 weekly boards), deleting older weekly rows. A used player record and its all-time best are removed 400 days after the last accepted run; a registration that never sends a run is removed after 30 days.

To limit abuse, the server uses a keyed hash of the request's network address. The address itself is not stored and the hash changes daily. The hourly sweep deletes rate-limit windows opened more than one hour ago, so each rate-limit row is kept for at most two hours. If a replay disagrees with the app, one diagnostic log line may contain only the app version and platform, with no player ID or run input.

You can turn off “Add my towers to this week's board” in Settings. Doltap then deletes your player and ranking rows. If you are offline, the deletion remains on your device and is sent before any later board action.

Player chat (iPhone 1.4.0 and later, Android 1.4.1 and later)

Doltap includes a player chat shared with other Erek Studio games, such as Orbion: one server and one account for all of them. Nothing below happens unless you open the chat, accept the community rules, agree to the AI safety check, and sign in — with Apple on iPhone, with your Google account on Android. You can play Doltap without it. The chat is for adults only: 18 or older, and 19 or older in Korea.

  • Sign in with Apple (iPhone). We store only the identifier Apple gives us for your account. If Apple includes your email address, we do not store it.
  • Sign in with Google (Android). We store only the identifier Google gives us for your Google account. Google's sign-in token also carries your email address, name and profile picture; we do not read, store or log them.
  • Sessions. Sign-in sessions are stored on the server only as hashes and expire after 90 days.
  • Your chat profile. A nickname (required) and your birth year (required, never shown to anyone, used only to confirm you are an adult). Optional: a display name, country, city, gender and the languages you speak, an avatar picked from a built-in set, and a short “about” line. Gender is never shown to other players; it is used only if you choose “only people of my gender can message me”. City appears only on your full profile.
  • Your Doltap card. Next to your nickname, other players see your best tower height and how many stones are on your wish cairn.
  • Messages. What you post in rooms and send in one-to-one messages is stored on Cloudflare's network so it can be delivered. Message text is deleted after 180 days.
  • Safety checks. Every message is checked by automatic rules and by an AI model from Anthropic (Claude), which classifies it for harassment, sexual content, threats, scams and sharing contact details. Anthropic processes the text only to return that classification and, under its commercial terms, does not use it to train its models. You are asked to agree to this, separately from the community rules, before you can use the chat.
  • Reports. If someone reports a message, we keep a copy of it and the five messages around it for up to 365 days to handle the report and meet legal obligations.
  • Profile photo (Chat+ only, optional). If you add one, the app crops it to a 512×512 square and re-encodes it without its location and camera data before sending it. Before anyone can see it, it is checked by the same Anthropic AI model for nudity, children, violence, hate and contact details; a photo that fails is deleted at once. Approved photos are stored on Cloudflare's network and shown on your chat profile while your Chat+ is active. Removing the photo or deleting your chat account deletes it. Doltap uses your photo library or camera only when you pick or take this photo.
  • Player rooms. Chat+ members can create rooms of their own, public or private (entered with an invite code). We store the room's name, optional description, language, owner, members and the people its owner removed. A room's name and description are checked by the same rules and Anthropic AI model before a public room is listed, and anyone who can see a room can report it. A room with no messages for 14 days is archived. Player rooms are not available in Korea mode.
  • Notifications. If you allow them, we store your device's push token — on Android, its Firebase Cloud Messaging registration ID — to tell you about new messages. Notifications are delivered by Apple's push service on iPhone and by Google's Firebase Cloud Messaging on Android. A notification never contains message text or the sender's name. A token that stops working is deleted.

Other players see your nickname, display name, avatar or photo, country, languages, Doltap card and what you post. They never see your birth year, gender, email, or Apple or Google identifier.

Korea mode. If your App Store or Google Play country, or your device region, is Korea, the chat runs in Korea mode, in every Erek Studio game: you see only Doltap's Korean room, one-to-one messages are possible only between people who have both written in that room, and there is no list of other players. Once an account is in Korea mode, it stays in Korea mode.

Deleting your chat account: in the chat, open Settings → Delete chat account, or ask by e-mail as described on the account deletion page. Your profile, sign-in link, push tokens and message text are deleted immediately. Report copies described above may remain for up to 365 days. A Chat+ purchase record (transaction identifier or Google Play purchase token, product, expiry) stays without a link to any account, so the subscription can still be restored. Your Doltap progress is not affected. Deleting the account does not cancel a Chat+ subscription; cancel it in your Apple ID settings or in Google Play › Subscriptions.

Chat+ purchases

Chat+ is bought through the App Store on iPhone and through Google Play on Android. We never see your name, email or payment details. To unlock it, our server confirms the purchase with Apple's App Store Server API or with the Google Play Developer API.

  • If you are signed into the chat, we store the transaction's identifier, the product and the expiry date with your chat account, so Chat+ works in every Erek Studio game where you sign in.
  • If you buy Chat+ in Doltap without a chat account, the app sends the App Store's signed record of the purchase to our server, which asks Apple whether it is active and answers with its expiry date. The server stores nothing from that request; to limit abuse it counts requests under a keyed hash of the network address, never the address itself. If you sign into the chat later, the purchase is stored with your account as above.
  • On Android, if you are signed into the chat, we store the purchase token Google Play issues (and a hash of it), the product and the expiry date with your chat account, and our server acknowledges the purchase to Google Play. Google Play tells our server when a subscription renews, is cancelled or is refunded, and the server then reads its status again from Google.
  • If you buy Chat+ in Doltap on Android without a chat account, the app sends Google Play's purchase token to our server, which asks Google Play whether the subscription is active and answers with its expiry date. The server stores nothing from that request and limits abuse the same way as above. If you sign into the chat later, the purchase is stored with your account.

Advertising

Version 1.3.0 and later: none. Doltap shows no ads, contains no advertising SDK, does not use an advertising identifier, and does not ask to track you.

Earlier versions

Version 1.3.0 used Apple Game Center or Google Play Games for separate platform leaderboards. Version 1.3.1 removes both integrations and replaces them with the optional shared board described above.

Versions before 1.3.0 included Google AdMob. Until you update from one of those versions, Google may process an advertising identifier, approximate region, device and app information, and ad interactions under Google's privacy policy. Updating removes the advertising and its SDK.

Network & third parties

The physics, wish, tower, and journal work on your device. Doltap contacts Erek Studio's board service only for the optional board, and Erek Studio's chat service only for the optional chat and to confirm a Chat+ purchase. Both services run on Cloudflare's network. For the chat, Erek Studio uses Anthropic for the safety checks described above, Apple for Sign in with Apple, purchase confirmation and push notifications on iPhone, and Google for Sign in with Google, purchase confirmation and Firebase Cloud Messaging notifications on Android. The operating system's store service is contacted when you buy or restore. There are no advertising, analytics, or social SDKs.

Sharing & Photos

If you choose to share a keepsake card or keep your tower as a wallpaper, the image is created on your device and handed to the operating system's standard share sheet. You choose where it goes; Doltap does not send it anywhere on its own. Where “Save Image” is available, Doltap asks only for permission to add the image and cannot read your photo library. The chat's profile photo is described in Player chat.

Purchases

The one-time unlock is processed by your device's store service. Doltap never sees your name, email, store credentials, or payment details; it receives only the entitlement result needed to unlock the features. From version 1.4.0 on iPhone and 1.4.1 on Android, Doltap no longer sells it; if you bought it, you keep everything it unlocks, and Restore keeps working. Chat+ is described in Chat+ purchases.

Children

Doltap is not directed at children. It has no ads or tracking. The optional shared board uses only a stone number and verified gameplay data; it does not collect a name or contact information. The player chat is for adults only: a birth year that does not show the player is an adult closes the chat for that account.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page. Version 1.3.1 replaced the separate platform leaderboards with one optional shared weekly board. Version 1.4.0 adds the optional player chat and the Chat+ subscription on iPhone; version 1.4.1 brings both to Android.
