Privacy Policy

Doltap · 돌탑 · Erek Studio

Short version: Doltap has no accounts and we log nothing about you. Everything you do, including your wish, stays on your device. There are no ads and no advertising, analytics or tracking SDK of any kind — free or bought.

What we collect

We — Erek Studio — do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. There is no sign-up, no profile, and no account. There is no exception: the app links no advertising, analytics, attribution or crash-reporting SDK, so there is no third party receiving anything about you either.

Data stored on your device

Doltap saves a few things locally so the app remembers your play:

  • Your one-word wish, your wish-cairn count, and your best tower height.
  • Your selected season (stone set).
  • Whether you have purchased the one-time Seasons unlock.

This information never leaves your device and is removed if you delete the app.

Advertising

There is none. Version 1.1 of Doltap did serve ads through Google AdMob — a short ad between runs, and an optional ad you could watch to carry a locked season stone set through a single run. That layer was removed in full on 26 August 2026: the AdMob SDK, the Google consent form (UMP), the iOS tracking prompt, the Android advertising-ID permission and the SKAdNetwork declarations are all gone from the app.

The season stone sets, the wish journal and the tower widget are opened by the one-time Seasons unlock, and by nothing else.

Network & third parties

The game runs entirely on your device: the physics, your wish, and your best tower need no connection. The only network request Doltap can make is the App Store or Google Play purchase check for the Seasons unlock, which is why the Android build declares the internet permission. It no longer declares the advertising-ID permission at all. Doltap bundles no advertising, analytics, attribution, or crash-reporting SDKs.

Sharing

If you choose to share a keepsake card of your best tower, the image is created on your device and handed to your operating system's standard share sheet. You pick where it goes; Doltap does not send it anywhere on its own.

Purchases

The optional Seasons unlock is processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. Doltap never sees your name, email, or payment details — those are handled entirely by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies.

Children

Doltap is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, including children. There is no advertising, so nothing in the app can lead a child to an external advertiser's site.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
