Privacy Policy

Doltap · 돌탑 · Erek Studio

Short version: Doltap has no ads, analytics, or tracking. Your wish and journal stay on your device. From version 1.3.1, Doltap has one shared weekly board. Nothing is sent to it until you choose to join. Your runs appear under a five-digit stone number, not a name, and you can leave and delete your board data from Settings.

What we collect

If you choose to join the shared board, Erek Studio processes a server-issued install ID, a five-digit stone number, and your verified best tower heights. A finished run is sent as its stone order and tap timing so our server can replay it. The replay input is processed in memory and is not stored. The board does not receive your name, email, wish, journal, store account, advertising identifier, or payment information.

Doltap contains no advertising, analytics, attribution, or crash-reporting SDK. It does not track you or use board data for advertising or analytics.

Data stored on your device

Doltap saves a few things locally so the app remembers your play:

  • Your one-word wish, wish-cairn count, and best tower height.
  • Your wish journal: the date, height, wish, and stone-order number of recent runs.
  • Your season, sound, haptics, board choice, and rating-prompt timing.
  • Whether you have purchased the one-time unlock.
  • From version 1.3.1, how many of your free runs you have used, and whether this device was already playing before 1.3.1. This count is never uploaded.
  • If you join the board, its install credential, stone number, confirmed bests, and any run or deletion waiting for a connection.

This local information is removed if you delete the app. Your wish and journal are never uploaded to the shared board.

Shared board (version 1.3.1 and later)

The board combines participating players on one weekly list. Joining is optional. The first time you join, the server issues a random install ID, secret, and five-digit stone number. The secret stays on your device; the server stores only its SHA-256 hash. The ID links your weekly best and your best height since the shared board began.

For each eligible finished run, Doltap sends the week's stone-order seed, tap ticks, and claimed height. The server replays the run and ranks only the height its own replay reaches. The seed and tap ticks are not stored on the server. The daily sweep at 04:00 UTC keeps the current week and the previous 11 weeks (12 weekly boards), deleting older weekly rows. A used player record and its all-time best are removed 400 days after the last accepted run; a registration that never sends a run is removed after 30 days.

To limit abuse, the server uses a keyed hash of the request's network address. The address itself is not stored and the hash changes daily. The hourly sweep deletes rate-limit windows opened more than one hour ago, so each rate-limit row is kept for at most two hours. If a replay disagrees with the app, one diagnostic log line may contain only the app version and platform, with no player ID or run input.

You can turn off “Add my towers to this week's board” in Settings. Doltap then deletes your player and ranking rows. If you are offline, the deletion remains on your device and is sent before any later board action.

Advertising

Version 1.3.0 and later: none. Doltap shows no ads, contains no advertising SDK, does not use an advertising identifier, and does not ask to track you.

Earlier versions

Version 1.3.0 used Apple Game Center or Google Play Games for separate platform leaderboards. Version 1.3.1 removes both integrations and replaces them with the optional shared board described above.

Versions before 1.3.0 included Google AdMob. Until you update from one of those versions, Google may process an advertising identifier, approximate region, device and app information, and ad interactions under Google's privacy policy. Updating removes the advertising and its SDK.

Network & third parties

The physics, wish, tower, and journal work on your device. In version 1.3.1, Doltap contacts Erek Studio's board service only for the optional board. The operating system's store service is contacted when you buy or restore the unlock. There are no advertising, analytics, or social SDKs.

Sharing & Photos

If you choose to share a keepsake card or keep your tower as a wallpaper, the image is created on your device and handed to the operating system's standard share sheet. You choose where it goes; Doltap does not send it anywhere on its own. Where “Save Image” is available, Doltap asks only for permission to add the image and cannot read your photo library.

Purchases

The optional one-time unlock is processed by your device's store service. Doltap never sees your name, email, store credentials, or payment details. It receives only the entitlement result needed to unlock the features.

Children

Doltap is not directed at children. It has no ads or tracking. The optional shared board uses only a stone number and verified gameplay data; it does not collect a name or contact information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page. Version 1.3.1 replaced the separate platform leaderboards with one optional shared weekly board.
