Privacy Policy

Doltap · 돌탑 · Erek Studio

Short version: Doltap has no accounts and we log nothing about you. Your wish and your towers stay on your device. From version 1.3.0 there are no ads at all. In 1.3.0 and later, if you are signed in to Game Center (iPhone, iPad) or Google Play Games (Android), your tower heights go to the game's leaderboards there, handled by Apple or Google. Versions before 1.3.0 show ads from Google AdMob — see “Earlier versions” below.

What we collect

We — Erek Studio — do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. There is no sign-up, no profile, and no account of ours. From version 1.3.0 Doltap contains no advertising, analytics, attribution, or crash-reporting SDK, and it does not track you. (Earlier versions contain Google's advertising SDK; see “Earlier versions”.)

Data stored on your device

Doltap saves a few things locally so the app remembers your play:

  • Your one-word wish, your wish-cairn count, and your best tower height.
  • Your wish journal: the date, height and wish of your recent runs, and the number of the stone sequence each was played with.
  • Your season, sound and haptics settings.
  • Whether you have purchased the one-time unlock.

This information never leaves your device and is removed if you delete the app.

Leaderboards (version 1.3.0 and later)

Doltap has weekly and all-time leaderboards on Apple Game Center and Google Play Games. They are used only if you are signed in to that service; Doltap never creates an account for you. When you are, the height of each finished run is sent to Doltap's leaderboards under your Game Center or Play Games player profile, and the app reads your weekly rank to show it on the card after a run. Other players can see leaderboard entries (player name and height). Apple and Google process this data under their own privacy policies — Apple, Google — and you can manage your profile and its visibility in their settings. If you are not signed in, nothing is sent.

Advertising

Version 1.3.0 and later: none. Doltap shows no ads and contains no advertising SDK. It does not use the advertising identifier (the Android build removes that permission) and does not ask to track you.

Earlier versions (before 1.3.0)

Until you update, an earlier version of Doltap shows a short ad between runs, never during one, served by Google AdMob. To do that, Google processes your advertising identifier, an approximate region derived from your IP address, device and app information, and your interaction with the ad, under Google's privacy policy. We only ever see aggregate revenue reporting — never anything about an individual person. In the EEA, Switzerland, and the UK, a Google consent form asks for your choice before the first ad loads; on iOS the system additionally asks whether the app may track you. In those versions the one-time unlock removes the ads. Updating to 1.3.0 removes the advertising and its SDK for everyone.

Network & third parties

The game itself runs on your device: the physics, your wish, your towers and your journal need no connection. From version 1.3.0, network requests come only from the leaderboards above when you are signed in, from the Google Play Games library that provides them on Android, and from the App Store or Google Play when you buy or restore the unlock. Earlier versions also make the advertising requests described above.

Sharing & Photos

If you choose to share a keepsake card of your best tower, or keep your tower as a wallpaper, the image is created on your device and handed to your operating system's standard share sheet. You pick where it goes; Doltap does not send it anywhere on its own. On iPhone and iPad, choosing “Save Image” there asks for permission to add photos — Doltap cannot see or read your photo library.

Purchases

The optional one-time unlock is processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. Doltap never sees your name, email, or payment details — those are handled entirely by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies.

Children

Doltap is not directed at children. It collects no data of its own, and from version 1.3.0 it shows no ads (earlier versions show ads, and an ad can lead to an external site). Leaderboards are Apple's and Google's services, with their own settings for who may use them.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page. Version 1.3.0 removed all advertising and added the leaderboards.
