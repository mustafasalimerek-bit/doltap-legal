Privacy Policy

Doltap · 돌탑 · Erek Studio

Short version: Doltap has no accounts and we log nothing about you. Everything you do, including your wish, stays on your device. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google processes device data to serve them. The one-time Seasons unlock removes the ads, and for buyers the ad SDK is never started.

What we collect

We — Erek Studio — do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. There is no sign-up, no profile, and no account. The one exception is the advertising in the free version, where Google processes data we never see or store; see “Advertising” below.

Data stored on your device

Doltap saves a few things locally so the app remembers your play:

  • Your one-word wish, your wish-cairn count, and your best tower height.
  • Your selected season (stone set).
  • Whether you have purchased the one-time Seasons unlock.

This information never leaves your device and is removed if you delete the app.

Advertising

Free play is ad-supported: a short ad between runs, plus an optional ad you can choose to watch to carry a locked season stone set through a single run. Ads are served by Google AdMob. To do that, Google processes your advertising identifier, an approximate region derived from your IP address, device and app information, and your interaction with the ad, under Google's privacy policy. We only ever see aggregate revenue reporting — never anything about an individual person.

In the EEA, Switzerland, and the UK, a Google consent form (UMP) asks for your choice before the first ad loads. You can change it at any time from Settings → Privacy options in the app. On iOS the system additionally asks whether the app may track you across apps and websites; if you decline, you still see ads, but they are not personalized.

The one-time Seasons unlock removes advertising completely. For buyers the ad SDK is never initialized, and neither the consent form nor the iOS tracking prompt appears.

Network & third parties

The game itself runs on your device: the physics, your wish, and your best tower need no connection. Network requests come only from the advertising in the free version, which is why the Android build now holds the internet and advertising-ID permissions. Doltap bundles no analytics, attribution, or crash-reporting SDKs.

Sharing

If you choose to share a keepsake card of your best tower, the image is created on your device and handed to your operating system's standard share sheet. You pick where it goes; Doltap does not send it anywhere on its own.

Purchases

The optional Seasons unlock is processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. Doltap never sees your name, email, or payment details — those are handled entirely by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies.

Children

Doltap is not directed at children and collects no data of its own. The free version does show ads, however, and an ad can lead to an external site. The one-time Seasons unlock removes advertising for anyone who would rather play without it.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
