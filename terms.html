The game and the free runs

Doltap is licensed to you, not sold. Every player gets 20 free runs; a run counts once its tower reaches three stones. From version 1.4.0 this includes players who stacked before version 1.3.1 and had unlimited runs until then: their 20 runs start with the update. After the free runs, starting a run needs paid play: on iPhone, Chat+ or the one-time unlock bought earlier; on Android, the one-time unlock. Runs stacked with paid play are not counted, so if your Chat+ ends, the free runs you had left when it began are still yours.

The one-time unlock

“The whole tower” is a single, non-consumable purchase. It is not a subscription: it is charged once and never renews. It unlocks unlimited runs, seeing the weekly rankings, the seasons (including Dawn), the temple sounds, the wish tag, the whole wish journal and the wallpaper.

From version 1.4.0 the iPhone app no longer sells it. Everyone who bought it keeps everything it unlocks, for good. It is tied to your App Store account; Restore purchases brings it back on another device with the same Apple ID. The Android app still sells it and restores it from Google Play.

The player chat (iPhone)

From version 1.4.0, Doltap on iPhone includes a player chat shared with other Erek Studio games, such as Orbion: one server, one account, signed in with Apple. It is optional and for adults only: 18 or older, and 19 or older in Korea. By using it you agree to the community rules, and you agree, separately, that your messages are checked for safety by Anthropic's AI, as described in the privacy policy.

There is zero tolerance for objectionable content and abusive users: messages that break the rules are removed and the accounts that send them are suspended or banned. Every message and profile can be reported, and anyone can be blocked; what you report is hidden for you at once. Reports are acted on within 24 hours.

Rooms are per game and language. A first message to someone new arrives as a request; you can send another once they reply. Without Chat+, you can send 5 one-to-one messages and read 5 received one-to-one messages per day (UTC); messages received beyond that stay locked until you have Chat+. Rooms are not limited.

Phone numbers, email addresses, links and accounts on other apps are never shared in rooms or profiles; messages that try are not delivered. In a one-to-one chat, a Chat+ member can share them once the other person has written at least three messages in that chat, unless that person has turned this off in the chat's settings. Never send money, gift cards or codes to anyone in the chat.

Korea mode. If your App Store country or device region is Korea, the chat runs in Korea mode in every Erek Studio game: only Doltap's Korean room, one-to-one messages only between people who have both written in that room, and no list of other players.

You can delete your chat account at any time in the chat: Settings → Delete chat account. The Android app has no chat.

Chat+ subscription (iPhone)

Chat+ is an auto-renewing subscription, monthly or yearly, at the price the App Store shows before you buy. In Doltap it unlocks:

  • unlimited runs and everything the one-time unlock holds: the weekly rankings, the seasons (including Dawn), the temple sounds, the wish tag, the whole wish journal and the wallpaper;
  • in the chat: unlimited one-to-one messages (sending and reading), looks for your profile, a profile photo, and sharing contact details in one-to-one chats as described above; outside Korea, also the country and language filters of the player list, and creating rooms of your own (public, or private with an invite code — up to two open at a time).

One Chat+ for every Erek Studio game. Chat+ belongs to your chat account and works in every Erek Studio game where you sign into the chat with that account, whichever game you bought it in. You can also buy it in Doltap without a chat account: it then unlocks Doltap's perks on that device, and when you sign into the chat later it moves to your chat account. A Chat+ already tied to one chat account cannot be moved to another. Fair-use limits apply to prevent spam.

Payment and renewal. Payment is charged to your Apple ID when you confirm the purchase. The subscription renews automatically at the same price unless auto-renew is turned off at least 24 hours before the end of the current period; your account is charged for the renewal within the 24 hours before the period ends. You can manage or cancel Chat+ at any time in your Apple ID's subscription settings; after cancelling, it stays active until the end of the period you have paid for. Deleting the app or your chat account does not cancel it.

Refunds are handled by Apple under its own policies, not by us; you can request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

When Chat+ ends, its perks stop: the free-run limit applies again (with the free runs you had left when Chat+ began), the profile photo is hidden and messages beyond the daily limit stay locked. Anything you own through the one-time unlock is not affected.

The shared board

On the board you appear under a five-digit stone number. Runs are replayed on our server from their stone order and tap timing; only the height the replay reaches is ranked, and entries that appear manipulated may be deleted. Joining is optional and never needs a purchase.

Availability

The game itself works offline. The shared board, the player chat and Chat+ confirmation depend on network services that may be unavailable, changed, or discontinued. Everything else keeps working when they are.
