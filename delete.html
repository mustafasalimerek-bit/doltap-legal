This page is for Doltap (돌탑), a game by Erek Studio. Doltap's optional player chat (iPhone from version 1.4.0, Android from version 1.4.1) uses a chat account, shared with Orbion, another Erek Studio game. The game itself has no account. You can delete the chat account in the app, or ask us by e-mail if you no longer have the app.

In the app

Open Doltap and go to Player chat → Settings → Delete chat account, then confirm. (The home screen's “Go to chat” also opens the player chat.) The account is deleted at once, as described below. Because the chat account is shared, this also deletes it for Orbion.

By e-mail, without the app

E-mail mustafasalimerek@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my Doltap chat account”. Write your chat nickname and whether you signed in with Apple (iPhone) or with Google (Android). We may ask you to confirm that the account is yours before we delete it. We delete it usually within a few days and at the latest within 30 days of your request, and we tell you by e-mail when it is done. The deletion is the same as the in-app one.

What is deleted at once

  • Your chat profile: nickname, display name, birth year, country, city, gender, languages, avatar and “about” line.
  • Your profile photo, including the stored image.
  • The link to your Apple or Google sign-in, your sign-in sessions, and the push tokens of your devices (on Android, the Firebase Cloud Messaging registration ID).
  • The text of every message you sent, in rooms and in one-to-one chats; your one-to-one conversations close.
  • Player rooms you created (they close, and their names and descriptions are removed) and your place in other people's rooms.
  • Your blocks, your Doltap card in the chat, and the counters behind the daily message limits.

Your Doltap game progress — wish, journal, best tower, runs and any purchase — is not affected: it lives on your device and in your store account.

What stays, and for how long

  • Report copies. If someone reported one of your messages, we keep a copy of it and the five messages around it for up to 365 days, to handle the report and meet legal obligations. Then they are deleted.
  • Chat+ purchase records. The store's transaction identifier (on Android, Google Play's purchase token), the product and the expiry date stay without a link to any account, so a running subscription can still be restored.
  • Bans and age blocks. If the account was banned, or its birth year did not show an adult, a one-way hash of its Apple or Google identifier stays, so the same sign-in cannot open a new chat account. The hash cannot be turned back into the identifier.
  • An anonymous marker that a deleted account existed, with no profile or contact data, so other people's closed conversations stay consistent.

Subscriptions

Deleting the chat account does not cancel a Chat+ subscription. Cancel it in Google Play › Subscriptions on Android, or in your Apple ID settings on iPhone.

Board data

The shared weekly board is separate from the chat and has no account. To delete your board data, turn off “Add my towers to the board” in Doltap's Settings: the app deletes your player and ranking rows. A board record is also removed automatically 400 days after its last accepted run (30 days for a registration that never sent a run). Details are in the privacy policy.
